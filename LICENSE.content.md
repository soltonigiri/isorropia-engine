# SCP-derived content license

Files under `data/` contain metadata and structured descriptions derived from the SCP Wiki. They are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

Per-page titles, source URLs, authors, and revisions are recorded in `data/manifest.json`. Reusers must preserve attribution and distribute adaptations under a compatible ShareAlike license.
